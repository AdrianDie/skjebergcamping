Location

Skjeberg Camping is a cosy family campsite with a good standard, idyllically located at the bottom of Skjebergkilen, just a short drive from Fredrikstad, Sarpsborg, Halden and Sweden. We have 15 pitches for caravans, campervans or motorhomes, 3-5 tent pitches and 25 well-equipped cabins, 1 house and 1 apartment.

View in Google Maps

Things to do

It’s a short distance to a range of activities, such as a great miniature golf course. If you want to enjoy warm days of sunbathing and swimming, you’re only a short distance from one of the finest sandy beaches Østfold has to offer. There’s space for various activities on the large lawn where you can also play beach volleyball.

Activity Centre

Dining options

Restaurant Salt, which is located next door, offers a good dining experience in beautiful natural surroundings. Nordic cuisine with inspiration from southern Europe – come and enjoy the beautiful view of Skjebergkilen with good food and drink. The restaurant’s menu is based on the best of what the season has to offer in terms of ingredients.

restaurant salt

Cabins

We have several types of cabins. 23 standard simple summer cabins with room for 4-6 people. Winter cabins with bathroom and kitchen for year-round use. 1 cosy house and 1 apartment.

Campsites

We have over 100 permanent campsites at Høysand and 80 at Revebukta. If you are interested in a permanent pitch, please send us an e-mail.

Discounts

For companies, people on assignment or if you need to rent for a longer period, we have good discounts.

Skjeberg is within easy reach of many things, with Nordby Shopping Centre just 15 minutes away and Strömstad 30 minutes away. From Strömstad there are ferries to the Koster islands.

Saltwater fishing is close by, where you’ll be able to catch a variety of different fish species in beautiful surroundings.

Petroglyphs along Oltidsveien, the viewing tower at Solberg.

Take a walk along the coastal path or cosy up on the beach. Sarpsborg is the city in Norway with the most days of sunshine per year.

We are located next door to Sporty minigolf. Skjeberg Golf Club is a 15-minute drive away.
Climbing on the border is 13 minutes away.

Other nearby attractions include Børtevann Canoe, Borgarsyssel Museum and Oyster Picking.
